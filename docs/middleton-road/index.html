About This Brew

A classic London porter with a deep mahogany colour and roasted character built from chocolate malt and roasted barley. Heritage crystal malt adds a touch of caramel sweetness, while Willamette and Centennial hops provide an earthy, floral backbone with firm bitterness. Clean and approachable at 5% ABV — the kind of porter you could drink all evening.
